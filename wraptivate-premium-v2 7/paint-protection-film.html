01 — PROTECTION THAT WORKS QUIETLY

Protection where
it matters.

Paint Protection Film (PPF) is a clear, durable film applied over suitable painted surfaces. It is designed to help reduce the effects of stone chips, road debris, insect marks and everyday surface wear on the panels it covers.

PPF is different from a colour-change wrap: its main purpose is paint protection, not a new colour or graphic design. Coverage can be planned around the areas most exposed to road spray and impacts, from a focused front-end package to selected high-impact panels or a broader brief.

Every vehicle is different. We’ll discuss your car, the condition of the paintwork, your driving habits and the level of coverage you have in mind before confirming what’s suitable.

03 — PPF OR VINYL WRAP?

Different films.
Different jobs.

Choose PPF when your priority is helping protect the paint underneath. Choose a vinyl colour-change wrap when your main aim is to change the appearance of the vehicle or introduce a new finish. They are not interchangeable, although a project may involve discussing more than one approach.

Product characteristics vary, so we’ll talk through the options available for your vehicle rather than promise a result that depends on a specific film or installation.
